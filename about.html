Our Story

Why We Started in Rewari, Haryana

In modern cities across Delhi NCR, finding food you can genuinely trust has become remarkably difficult. Most retail dairy is aggregated from countless anonymous milk collections, pasteurized repeatedly, and stripped of its natural character. Similarly, fresh produce often sits in wholesale mandis for days before reaching consumer kitchens.

We chose to do something fundamentally different. In Rewari, Haryana — just 45 minutes from Gurugram — we acquired and consolidated a contiguous 50-acre landholding to create a closed-loop farm and dairy model.

Here, we grow our own green fodder, cultivate crops like dragon fruit, mushrooms, grapes and kiwi, raise our dairy herd ethically, and operate our own small-scale dairy processing line.
