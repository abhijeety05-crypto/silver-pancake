In-House Processing Facility

Made With Care, Close to the Source.

We have established our own small-scale dairy production setup directly on the farm. By keeping the processing close to milking, we maintain complete control over milk handling, chilling, traditional churning, and hygienic packaging.

Our Hygiene & Quality Standard

Milk goes directly from sanitized milking points into a stainless-steel plate chiller at 4°C. Our A2 Ghee is slow-cooked in low batches from cultured curd butter, preserving natural granular texture and nutrients.

Touchless Milking Traditional Bilona Sealed Glass Jars
Stage 01

Milk Handling & Chilling

Immediate cooling to 4°C in food-grade stainless vats to preserve sweetness and prevent spoilage.

Stage 02

Natural Fermentation

Whole milk is gently boiled, cooled, and naturally set with pure starter culture for smooth curd.

Stage 03

Traditional Bilona Churning

Curd is churned in bi-directional churners to yield pure white makkhan and probiotic chaas.

Stage 04

Slow Ghee Preparation

White butter is slow-simmered over low flame until it forms clear, golden, granular A2 ghee.

Stage 05

Curd Setting & Packing

Daily fresh curd packed in clean, food-grade pots for optimal natural firmness.

Stage 06

Sterilized Glass Packaging

Filled and sealed into steam-washed glass containers with tamper-evident seals.
