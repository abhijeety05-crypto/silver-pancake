Farm Infrastructure

The 6 Zones of Our 50-Acre Ecosystem

Every acre of our farm is thoughtfully allocated to foster a self-sustaining cycle where agriculture and dairy support one another.

Zone 01 • 14 Acres

Dairy Herd & Open Grazing Paddock

Our dairy cattle are housed in high-roof, well-ventilated sheds with rubber flooring and cooling mist fans. They enjoy daily access to open paddocks for free movement and social interaction.

  • 🌱 Farm-Grown Fodder: Fresh sorghum, berseem, maize, and Napier grass grown on our own fields.
  • 💧 Clean Water: Automated RO & fresh groundwater drinking troughs available 24/7.
  • 🩺 Veterinary Oversight: Regular preventive health checkups and ethical livestock care.
Zone 02 • In-House Facility

Small-Scale Processing & Chilling Plant

Having our own small processing setup right on the farm means milk never sits warm. It is transferred immediately through closed sanitary lines into a stainless-steel plate chiller down to 4°C.

  • 🥛 Touchless Handling: Closed-loop food grade 304/316 stainless steel lines.
  • 🧈 Traditional Bilona: Bi-directional motorized churners for curd churning.
  • 🍾 Glass Bottling: Automated washing, steam sanitization, and tamper-evident sealing.
Zone 03 • 12 Acres

Dragon Fruit Trellis Orchards

Our dragon fruit block features reinforced concrete posts supporting vigorous red and white pitaya varieties. Rewari’s sunny climate combined with precision drip fertigation yields intensely flavorful, sweet fruit.

  • ☀️ Sun Ripened: Left on the plant until fully mature for rich brix sweetness.
  • 💧 Drip Irrigation: Water-efficient root-zone delivery powered by solar pumps.
  • 📦 Harvest Care: Hand-clipped and packed into protective foam fruit sleeves.
Zone 04 • Indoor Facility

Climate-Controlled Mushroom Houses

We grow white button mushrooms and oyster mushrooms in dedicated insulated chambers where temperature, humidity, and airflow are monitored around the clock.

  • 🍄 Daily Harvest: Picked at the button stage for firm texture and bright white caps.
  • 🌬️ Air Filtration: Clean HEPA-filtered air circulation prevents contamination.
  • ⏱️ Fast Transit: Packed and dispatched to NCR within 24 hours of plucking.
